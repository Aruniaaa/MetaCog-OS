FocusAI: Your Cognitve Discipline Engine

FocusAI is your discipline engine. It helps you plan your sessions, measure your focus, track interruptions, and actually stay in the work. You get accountabilty, phone-based distraction prevention, visualization of progress, time management tools. Basically, everything built around ensuring you get your work done instead of focusing just on aesthetics. It’s the part of MetaCog that keeps you consistent, stable, and intentional.

SaathiAI: Your Cognitive Training Companion

SaathiAI is your cognitive training companion. It strengthens your problem-solving skills without making you dependent on AI. Instead of giving you answers, Saathi guides your reasoning, points out conceptual gaps, and helps you think independently. You also get a Smart Quiz Generator for MCQs from any material, Context-aware STEM doubt guidance, Skill tracking & accuracy analytics. It’s built to reduce AI dependency and increase actual intelligence.
